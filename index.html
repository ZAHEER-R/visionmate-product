Everyday situations

VisionMate brings practical support to the moments where a little more information makes a difference.

One companion, many tasks

Everything it does

Eight connected areas of support, shaped around everyday needs.

01

Press-and-hold Talk

  • Use the visible Talk button by pressing and holding
  • Speak naturally and release to stop listening
  • Ask about tasks, routes, contacts, notes or reminders
  • Works with voice or typed commands
02

Voice agent

  • Ask questions in plain language
  • Open key features with speech commands
  • Hear spoken responses in your selected language
  • Choose device speech or Gemini voice output
03

Vision Lens

  • Start camera preview only when you choose
  • Switch front and rear camera and adjust zoom
  • Ask what is in frame or read printed text
  • Use the optional spoken explanation of the scene
04

Travel and location help

  • Share your current location and destination
  • Estimate distance, time and route information
  • View route geometry on an interactive map
  • Useful for everyday movement and planning
05

Translation

  • Translate text into supported languages
  • Use spoken language output for accessibility
  • Helpful for reading signs, documents and notices
  • Supports multilingual voice-first use
06

Notes, reminders and profile

  • Save reminders and personal notes
  • Keep recent chat history and settings
  • Manage profile details and language preferences
  • Use guest mode or optional account sync
07

Emergency support

  • Call a trusted contact with confirmation
  • Share your location during an emergency
  • Open a dedicated emergency screen quickly
  • Helpful when a person needs fast assistance
08

Simple phone controls

  • Bottom navigation for Back, Home, Go, Lens, Voice and Me
  • Large touch targets and screen focus for easier use
  • Designed for mobile browsers and installable PWA use
  • Lets the user stay in control of each action

Supported languages: English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and Punjabi. The app is designed for mobile use with a fixed bottom navigation and a press-and-hold Talk action.

How it works

You stay in control from the first request to the spoken guidance.

Safety and honesty

An aid, not a replacement.

VisionMate does not replace a white cane, guide dog or mobility training. Camera detection can miss things. Crossing help is information, not permission to cross. Confirm medicines and currency, and use the app in good lighting.
